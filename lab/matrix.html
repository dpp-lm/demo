Matrix orderings lab

The pool's DPP kernel L = diag(√w) G diag(√w), rows reordered so similar completions form blocks. Compare orderings side by side. Notes: docs/matrix-views.md, section 2A.

Hover a cell to see the pair.

Everything is computed from L, the kernel the DPP uses (w = κ for Most likely, κ/q for Sample; L = G for the Global target at T = ∞). Pool order: as drawn. Average linkage: Manuel's order (UPGMA on 1 − cos; the cosine of L is G, so it ignores quality). Pivots, flat: the first K pivots of pivoted Cholesky (greedy volume: the Most likely picks), each followed by the completions it explains the most of. Pivots, nested: the whole factorisation as a tree; parent = the earlier pivot that explains the most of a completion. Themes: the K-DPP chooses K eigenvectors of L, theme a with probability p_a, and every inclusion probability splits exactly over them, π_i = Σ_a p_a u_a(i)²; each completion joins the theme carrying the largest part. The bars beside the theme blocks show that part's share of π_i (full width at the dashed line = 100%): long bars mean a completion belongs to its block cleanly, short ones that its chance of being sampled is spread over many themes; "mean share" averages it over the pool. Red ticks: the greedy (Most likely) picks.
